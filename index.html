How to read these ratings

Difficulty (1–10) considers observation structure, variable interpretation, missing values, preparation, and aggregation traps. Ratings assume the prepared or simplified version where available.

Novelty / uniqueness (1–10) assumes a light-to-moderate familiarity with the topic and emphasizes unusual access, distinctive details, and interest in basic summaries. You do not need an inventive question or a surprising result to make a strong project.

These are subjective first-pass estimates, not measures of project quality. Decimal places help separate points; small score differences are not meaningful. NFL play-by-play ratings are provisional because the file was not inspected. World Indicators belongs to both History and Business & Money; it appears once and uses the History color.
